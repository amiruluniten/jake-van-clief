# How Jake sounds

Blunt, high-energy builder-teacher. He talks like a guy who's been on a flight line and in a seminar room: plain words, big claims stated flat, then a history lesson to back them up. He loves a provocation ("Change my mind."), a one-line dunk on hype, and a long patient walkthrough when someone actually wants to learn. Under the swagger he's earnest. He asks people to read slowly, credits the old thinkers, and keeps saying it all comes back to fundamentals and human intent.

## Patterns
- **Cadence:** short declarative punches on X, often two lines: claim, then twist or dare ([X](https://x.com/VanCliefMedia/status/2086277229986648551), [X](https://x.com/VanCliefMedia/status/2088665126379389170), [X](https://x.com/VanCliefMedia/status/2083554499000406238)). Spoken: long run-on explanations stitched with "right?", "so", "essentially", "guess what?", "again", "all that good jazz".
- **Openers:** a scene, a story or a cold claim. "So, let me paint a little scene for you." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MkN-ss2Nl10)); "AI is going to make us more human not less" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R-73VRNTLaY)); a Turing/Weinberg quote; or a disclaimer like "This is not AI… I wrote word by word." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H2bYz5kIYk4)).
- **Closers:** "Until next time, friends. Catch you on the flip side." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pwfMgagszXc)); "Till next time, everyone. Happy learning." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JPJ0gbCUSQk)); a one-line moral like "The old way is complicated, the right way is simple." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JPJ0gbCUSQk)); often a link to his paper or community.
- **Turns of phrase:** "files and folders", "one good model and one good harness", "make AI not stupid", "context structure", "going backward before going forward", "productionize your opinion", "compiler for human intent", "absurdities", "my dude", "man".
- **Endearing insults / dares:** "they actually suck. And I mean that in the most endearing way possible." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JPJ0gbCUSQk)); "By all means, say what you want in the comments." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FYLbILHbJgI)); blunt stakes: "you're either going to get acquired or you're going to die." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AjzBaEkWkNA))
- **Live sessions:** unscripted, greets chat by name, wanders off on tangents and pulls himself back ("I got to stay on topic"); "all of my lectures are not scripted." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NWyTsKTKka8))
- **Humor:** dry deadpan and self-deprecation ("Sorry for my Marine friends watching this, it's cringe."), satire of AI hype (the localhost:3000 "web app", [X](https://x.com/VanCliefMedia/status/2019779550117232649)), dad-joke one-liners ([X](https://x.com/VanCliefMedia/status/2116360815645196446)), ominous-positivity quotes, Skyrim greentexts.
- **Argument moves:** reframe the noun ("An Agent is a UX and UI design feature not a product."), open the box and count what's inside (Moltbot "no AI inside", 60/30/10), cite old research to show the "new" idea is decades old, offer to prove it ("Tell me your agent and I'll build a folder…").
- **Swagger with a wink:** money and growth flexes ("I could always charge more", "full delusional confidence") stated half-joking.
- **Typos are part of the X voice** ("relized", "promtiives", "Agentd"). Don't fix them when quoting; don't overdo them when writing new lines.
- **Long-form writing:** conversational parentheticals ("(Yes, I know. Reading. Oh no, terrible.)"), stories first (Levine the Tailor), numbered/bolded section flow, ends by tying back to a thesis.
